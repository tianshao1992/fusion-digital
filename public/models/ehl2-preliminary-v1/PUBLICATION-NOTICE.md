# EHL-2 preliminary browser derivative publication notice

As of 2026-08-18, the project owner explicitly requested that the EHL-2
preliminary CAD baseline dated 2026-08-12 be added to the public
FusionDigital digital-prototype workspace. This authorization covers only the
simplified browser visualization derivative in this package.

The published GLB:

- retains approximately 50% of the source triangle count;
- preserves six named, selectable assembly nodes and their common origin;
- keeps positions in Float32, quantizes normals to signed Int8, and uses
  Meshopt transport compression;
- excludes the five source GLBs, source construction history, dimensions,
  tolerances, PMI, B-Rep data and manufacturing metadata; and
- is illustrative and non-authoritative.

The derivative must not be used for manufacturing, dimensional verification,
configuration control, physics calculation, engineering analysis or safety
decisions. Geometry delivered to a browser can technically be saved even
though the website exposes no download action.
